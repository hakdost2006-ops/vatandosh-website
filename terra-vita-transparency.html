Budget framework

Six categories. One traceable record.

Every reported pilot expense will be assigned to a consistent category, helping donors compare spending with current shelter needs.

01

Food

Daily food for dogs, cats, the shelter donkey, and animals in temporary care.

02

Veterinary care

Exams, tests, imaging, medicines, urgent treatment, and recovery care.

03

Sterilization

Procedures that reduce future suffering and pressure on shelter capacity.

04

Vaccination & parasite treatment

Disease prevention and flea, tick, and parasite control for animals in care.

05

Sanitation & utilities

Cleaning, garbage removal, electricity, gas, water-system upkeep, and supplies.

06

Transport

Rescue trips, veterinary visits, and movement of animals and essential supplies.

How funds are used

From contribution to public record.

Vatandosh remains responsible for U.S. funds it receives. Terra Vita provides local care and monthly documentation.

Receive & designate

Vatandosh records U.S. gifts marked “Terra Vita.” Local gifts continue through Terra Vita’s established payment options.

Approve the need

Support is matched to urgent, eligible care needs and one of the six public budget categories.

Pay & document

Funds may support verified transfers, direct vendor payments, or documented reimbursements, depending on the situation.

Reconcile & publish

Vatandosh reviews available receipts, invoices, screenshots, photos, expense notes, and outcome counts before updating this portal.

Data standard

Clear labels are part of transparency.

“Partner-reported” means Terra Vita provided the figure. “Reviewed” will mean Vatandosh checked the supporting record. “Pending” means the information has not yet been received or reconciled.

Pilot limitation: This is not a live accounting system. Data is manually updated and may lag behind shelter activity. Financial totals will be based on Vatandosh’s designated-gift records and reviewed pilot documentation; local Terra Vita/Payme totals will be included only when a reliable reporting method is integrated.
